Frequently Asked Questions

When doesTechForge 2026 take place?

TechForge 2026 will take place from October 16 to October 18, 2026.

Where is the conference being held?

The conference will be held at theTechForge Convention Center in Algiers, Algeria.

No. Attendees can register for individual days or choose the full three-day conference pass.
