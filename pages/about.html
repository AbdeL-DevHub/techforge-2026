TechForge 2026

Where ideas become technology.

TechForge 2026 is a three-day technology conference bringing together developers, designers, engineers, entrepreneurs, and technology enthusiasts to explore the tools and ideas shaping the future of software.

About the conference

What is TechForge ?

TechForge is an annual technology conference focused on practical knowledge, emerging technologies, and the people building the next generation of digital products.
From frontend development and artificial intelligence to cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity, TechForge 2026 brings together experts from across the technology industry for three days of talks, workshops, demonstrations, and networking.

What to expect

  • Technical talks from experienced developers and engineers
  • Hands-on workshops
  • Discussions about artificial intelligence
  • Modern web development techniques
  • Developer tooling and productivity

Conference Themes

Web Development

Explore modern frontend architectures, responsive design, JavaScript, accessibility, and the evolving web platform.

Artificial Intelligence

Discover how developers are incorporating AI into applications and workflows, from APIs and assistants to AI-powered development tools.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Learn how modern applications are deployed, scaled, monitored, and maintained in cloud environments.

Future of Technology

Discuss emerging technologies and the trends that could shape software development over the coming years.
